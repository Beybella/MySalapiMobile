## WHICH ONE FOR EACH EMAIL TYPE?

### Singil (Loan Collection) 💰
**Recommended:** Option 1 or 4
- Needs professional, trustworthy appearance
- Important payment details must be clear
- Option 4's receipt style adds legitimacy

### Bill Reminder 📋
**Recommended:** Option 1 or 2
- Needs to be quick to read
- Amount should be prominent
- Option 2's bold approach works well for urgency

### Budget Shortfall Alert ⚠️
**Recommended:** Option 3 or 1
- Needs to show multiple bills
- Requires good information density
- Option 3's layout handles tables well

### Group Expense (Ambagan) 👥
**Recommended:** Option 1 or 2
- Should be friendly and casual
- Quick to understand
- Option 2's simplicity works for group communication
