## QUICK DECISION GUIDE

### Choose Option 1 if:
- ✅ You want safe, professional design
- ✅ You need something familiar to users
- ✅ You want easy maintenance
- ✅ Mobile responsiveness is priority

### Choose Option 2 if:
- ✅ You want bold, modern look
- ✅ Mobile-first is critical
- ✅ You prefer minimalism
- ✅ Quick scanning is priority

### Choose Option 3 if:
- ✅ You have lots of information
- ✅ Desktop users are primary
- ✅ You like structured layouts
- ✅ Icons enhance your brand

### Choose Option 4 if:
- ✅ Trust and legitimacy are key
- ✅ Users need records/receipts
- ✅ Formal tone is preferred
- ✅ Print-friendly is important
