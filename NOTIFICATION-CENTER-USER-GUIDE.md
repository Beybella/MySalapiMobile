# 🔔 MySalapi Notification Center - User Guide

## What is the Notification Center?

The Notification Center is your personal inbox for all bill reminders, loan alerts, and payment notifications in MySalapi. Never miss an important reminder again!

---

## 📱 How to Access

### From Home Screen
1. Look at the top-right corner of your home screen
2. You'll see a bell icon 🔔
3. If you have unread notifications, you'll see a red badge with a number
4. Tap the bell icon to open the Notification Center

---

## 🎯 What You Can Do

### View All Notifications
- See all your notifications organized by date:
  - **Today** - Notifications from today
  - **Yesterday** - Notifications from yesterday
  - **Earlier** - Older notifications

### Check Unread Count
- At the top, you'll see two numbers:
  - **Total** - How many notifications you have
  - **Unread** - How many you haven't checked yet

### Tap a Notification
- Tap any notification to:
  - **Mark it as read** (no more bold text)
  - **Go to the relevant screen** (bill, loan, or group)

### Delete a Notification
- See that ✕ button on the right of each notification?
- Tap it to remove that notification from your history
- You'll be asked to confirm before deleting

### Mark All as Read
- Too many unread notifications?
- Tap the **✓** icon in the top-right corner
- All notifications will be marked as read instantly

### Clear All History
- Want to start fresh?
- Tap the **trash icon** 🗑️ in the top-right corner
- Confirm to delete ALL notification history
- **Warning:** This cannot be undone!

### Refresh
- Pull down on the notification list to refresh
- Your notifications will reload

## 🎨 Understanding Notification Types

### 💰 Bill Reminders
**Icon:** Receipt  
**Color:** Green  
**What it means:** A bill payment is coming up or due  
**Tap to:** Go to your Budget tab to see the bill

**Example:**
```
💰 Bill Due Today
Your bill "Electric Bill" of ₱2,500.00
is due TODAY!
```

### 🤝 Loan Reminders
**Icon:** Handshake  
**Color:** Blue  
**What it means:** A loan payment is due (you owe or someone owes you)  
**Tap to:** See the loan details

**Example:**
```
🤝 Loan Reminder
Loan "Personal Loan" (₱5,000.00) is due
in 3 days - pay payment
```

### 💰 Payment Reminders (Singil)
**Icon:** Mail  
**Color:** Amber  
**What it means:** Someone sent you a payment reminder  
**Tap to:** See the loan and payment details

**Example:**
```
💰 Payment Reminder from Juan Dela Cruz
You have a payment reminder of ₱5,000.00
Check your email for payment details
```

### 🚨 Overdue Alerts
**Icon:** Alert circle  
**Color:** Red  
**What it means:** A bill or loan is past its due date  
**Tap to:** Go to the overdue item

**Example:**
```
🚨 Bill Overdue
"Credit Card" ₱8,500 is 2 day(s) overdue!
```

### 👥 Group Expenses
**Icon:** People  
**Color:** Mauve  
**What it means:** Someone shared a group expense with you  
**Tap to:** See the group details

### 💼 Budget Alerts
**Icon:** Wallet  
**Color:** Green-brown  
**What it means:** Important budget information  
**Tap to:** Go to your Budget tab

## 💡 Tips & Tricks

### Keep Your Inbox Clean
- Delete notifications you don't need anymore
- Use "Clear All" occasionally to start fresh
- Check notifications regularly to avoid clutter

### Use the Badge Count
- The red badge shows unread notifications
- It updates automatically
- Tap the bell when you see a badge!

### Understand Time Stamps
- **2m ago** = 2 minutes ago
- **3h ago** = 3 hours ago
- **1 day ago** = Yesterday
- **Aug 20** = Specific date (older notifications)

### Unread vs Read
- **Unread notifications** have:
  - Bold text
  - Green left border
  - A small dot on the right
- **Read notifications** look normal

## 🔍 What Notifications Are Saved?

The Notification Center automatically saves:

✅ Bill reminders (7 days, 3 days, due date)  
✅ Loan reminders (7 days, 3 days, due date)  
✅ Singil payment requests  
✅ Overdue alerts  
✅ Group expense notifications  
✅ Budget alerts  

**Note:** The app keeps your last 50 notifications. Older ones are automatically removed.

## ❓ Common Questions

### Q: I deleted a notification by accident. Can I get it back?
**A:** No, deleted notifications cannot be recovered. Be careful when using the delete button!

### Q: Why don't I see all my old notifications?
**A:** The app keeps only your last 50 notifications to save space. Older ones are automatically removed.

### Q: Can I turn off certain types of notifications?
**A:** Currently, all notification types are saved. You can delete individual notifications you don't want.

### Q: Does the Notification Center work offline?
**A:** Yes! Your notification history is saved on your device and works without internet.

### Q: What happens if I tap "Clear All"?
**A:** ALL notification history will be permanently deleted. You'll be asked to confirm before this happens.

### Q: Why is my badge count different from my notification count?
**A:** The badge shows only UNREAD notifications. The total count includes both read and unread.

## 📊 Empty State

When you have no notifications, you'll see:
- A notification icon with a slash through it
- Message: "No Notifications"
- Helpful text explaining what will appear here

This is normal when you first install the app or after clearing all history.

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### Badge Not Updating
- Pull down to refresh in Notification Center
- Close and reopen the app
- Check if you've marked notifications as read

### Notifications Not Appearing
- Make sure push notifications are enabled in Profile > Settings
- Check your device notification settings
- Restart the app

### Can't Delete a Notification
- Make sure you're tapping the ✕ button
- Confirm the deletion dialog
- Pull down to refresh
